Product requirements

Nuke ownership and defense

  • All nuclear prediction, retaliation, defensive interception, and duplicate-launch logic must classify only the user's own missiles as self-owned and missiles from permanent teammates as allied. A missile owned by any other player remains hostile, even while a breakable diplomatic alliance with that player is active.

Auto-Bot update persistence

  • Updating or reinjecting the userscript must preserve the user's persisted Auto-Bot master toggle. Runtime safety stops, including public-lobby blocking and the slow-tick watchdog, may stop the engine but must not save enabled=false as though the user disabled it.

Economic structure expansion

  • City and Factory placement must treat a border segment shared exclusively with friendly teammates or allies as part of the safe team interior. Unowned, hostile, and mixed ally/hostile border segments remain exposed. Safety against the exposed perimeter and real commercial-rail connectivity must determine placement; proximity to the geometric center of a single player's territory must not be used as a goal by itself.
  • When the planner wants a City, Factory, or Port, it must attempt a new structure before an upgrade. A bounded placement miss, timeout, missing build descriptor, or other indeterminate result must not authorize an upgrade. After the normal bounded placement search fails, the planner must incrementally inspect the complete owned territory across later construction decisions, examining no more than the normal 25-candidate placement budget in one decision. Existing economic structures may be upgraded only when that exhaustive geometric search completes against an unchanged territory/structure snapshot and finds no legal site; incomplete scans, changed snapshots, unsafe-only sites, and failed authoritative probes remain indeterminate and do not permit an upgrade. A no-space proof must never keep one Auto-Bot tick alive while it traverses the map.
  • Mature-game construction decisions must not rebuild identical global topology for every placement candidate. Factory trade destinations are shared across one construction decision, and an economic scan fingerprint may inspect only aggregate structure and border counts once per game tick. Authoritative buildability probes remain the final legality check when a count-preserving topology change makes an incremental scan snapshot stale.

SAM upgrade opportunity cost

  • A routine SAM level must be funded from surplus remaining after the next currently useful City, Factory, or Port action. Economy strength is therefore determined from the live productive opportunity and its real cost, not from a fixed gold, structure-count, or game-time threshold. An upgrade proven necessary to intercept an already incoming missile may bypass this reserve.

Defense Post portfolio coverage

  • During an active land attack, Defense Post demand must exclude threatened-front tiles already covered by an existing or in-progress Post. The engine applies at most one Post bonus per defended tile, so overlapping coverage has no marginal combat value. Within the existing threat-derived maximum, new placements must maximize newly covered threatened tiles inside the engine's live effect radius before applying secondary SAM-protection placement preferences. Pre-attack strategic deterrence retains its separate bounded policy.
  • During a live land invasion, a new Defense Post must use a placement with real territorial depth. The planner must not relax onto a shallow border tile when no depth-safe candidate is available, and equally useful candidates must prefer the one farthest behind the threatened front so the structure has the greatest chance to finish before the territory is captured.
  • If an incoming Nation or Human land army matches or exceeds our current troops, an affordable failed Defense Post placement must retain its construction budget for a bounded retry. It must not release that gold to a Port or unrelated structure while the overwhelming invasion remains live. The hard reserve remains active continuously, but an unchanged failed placement search must retry at most once per game second to avoid repeated full candidate probes and main-thread stutter. Failed placement under a smaller attack may still release the slot so ordinary economy cannot be starved indefinitely.

Auto-fire collateral accounting

  • When choosing an atom impact that must cover a hostile SAM or Silo, Auto Fire Building must exclude collateral structures already inside the blast radius of any active own, allied, or otherwise guaranteed in-flight missile. The impact scorer should maximize additional hostile structures destroyed while preserving coverage of the primary target.

Spawn selection

  • When the user provides a screenshot, grid reference, or named map location to illustrate a spawn-selection problem, you must treat it as an example unless the user explicitly requests location-specific behavior. Improve the general spawn-selection objective across maps; do not hard-code the pictured map, coordinate, grid cell, or nearby territories.
  • In team games, the bounded initial candidate budget must be sampled inside the geometry formed by committed teammate spawns before ranking. Sampling the whole map and discarding exterior points afterward must not collapse a valid team region to a single candidate. The selected position and every reported alternative must remain inside that geometry.

Tribe capture reinforcement

  • You must keep every live outbound tribe attack in the attack-candidate set, even when the tribe is temporarily absent from nearby(). Compare the live committed troops with the current Defense Post-aware robust conquest floor (four times the live tribe force) on every decision pass. Two times the tribe force is only the engine's legal admission threshold and must not be treated as sufficient for conquest. Reconcile and add target-specific pending intents instead of treating their mere existence as sufficient. When the combined visible and pending force is underfunded, emit only the missing reinforcement; when it already meets the floor, do not emit another attack. Reinforcement of an active underfunded conquest takes priority over starting a new Terra Nullius intent.

Time-aware tribe clusters

  • Every new tribe conquest must enter through a live candidate carrying finite cluster size and access scores. Generic weak-target, victim, ally-assist, random-neighbour, and ordinary fallback strategies must not bypass this admission rule. A forced response to an incoming tribe attack and a target-specific reinforcement of an already active conquest remain permitted without a fresh cluster score.
  • Tribe-cluster targeting must value only the portion of a connected tribe chain that is likely to remain edible when the bot reaches it. Rival attacks and observed expansion pressure must be compounded with the number of prerequisite tribe captures. A tribe predicted to be captured before arrival must not contribute cluster value, act as a gateway to tribes behind it, occupy the bounded strategic sample, or appear in the preferred-cluster overlay.

Early-game frame budget

  • An accepted personal spawn must not initialize or reserve Terra Nullius attacks while the match remains in the global spawn phase. The protected opening burst begins on the first playable tick, then may pipeline its bounded intents without waiting for prior free-land commands to settle, up to the aggregate opening limit and never through the retained troop reserve. Once no passable adjacent Terra Nullius remains, the fast lane must yield immediately to tribe and other strategic planning even when a nominal fragment of the opening allowance remains unused. Once every opening free-land intent and visible wave has resolved, expansion must immediately continue under the live acceleration/defense reserve for the remainder of the bounded opening window. It must not wait for a capacity-derived future production peak that exceeds the current army. After that window, the adaptive production/threat reserve becomes authoritative.
  • Consecutive opening-expansion decisions must reuse the complete tribe-border topology for a short, bounded game-tick window, invalidating immediately when the live tribe-id set changes. Cluster opportunity traversal should start only from actionable candidates, and full border scans must yield cooperatively so no synchronous slice monopolizes a render frame. The frequent Terra Nullius liveness lane must not invoke the complete strategic tribe planner; it may only reconcile active conquests and emit one adjacent free-land intent. Pending tribe or Nation attacks must not pause this lane; only an unreconciled Terra Nullius intent may defer its next free-land emission. Publishing an unchanged preferred cluster must not invalidate the shared overlay canvas, and a data-driven cluster layer must not register a periodic no-op scan.
  • A transient empty actionable or nearby-tribe selector must not clear a published preferred cluster while every published member is still a live hostile tribe. The cluster must be cleared only after its published membership is no longer live, and that visual removal must wake the shared overlay scheduler exactly once. Refreshing the synchronous attack snapshot must preserve the independently owned preferred-cluster fields while asynchronous tribe planning is pending.
  • Structure policy must not synchronously construct full-map water-component labels merely to answer whether the player has shared water. Direct ocean access must be decided from the cached live border in bounded work. Exact global labeling remains available and may run lazily only when an inland lake requires the trusted-partner test.
  • The static reverse-maritime distance field used for tribe routing must begin preparing in bounded slices after spawn. A tribe decision may synchronously finish only the remaining work, and no partial field may be published or used for ranking. Incremental and one-shot construction must produce the exact same distance field.
  • Cooperative water-route estimation must make reachability decisions from deterministic route length and visited-node limits, not from machine speed. Wall-clock deadlines may be used only as explicit secondary safety fuses; yielding between bounded batches remains mandatory.

Diplomacy intent priority

  • Combat decisions must run before routine embargo maintenance. A large hostile roster must be drained through a bounded embargo quota across later decisions, stopping before the shared client intent budget is exhausted and preserving capacity for defense, construction, combat, or donations. Routine diplomacy must not knowingly emit intents that the local rate limiter will reject.

Incoming-attack priority

  • A non-overwhelming land invasion must be interdicted from the surplus above the live production reserve and pending outbound commitments. The complete army of an unrelated border opponent must not be counted again as a defensive hold for this cancellation action. The emitted payload must be capped to the smaller of that safe surplus and the troops currently incoming from the selected attacker, including when the attacker is a tribe.
  • Any live hostile incoming attack, including one from a tribe, must preempt new offensive troop spending. Retaliation is a defensive action and must not depend on the expansion toggle. A failed defensive emission must hold the decision instead of falling through to Terra Nullius or tribe expansion. The opening-expansion fast lane must detect the threat before waiting on any pending outbound intent, terminate its follow-up chain, and give both defensive construction and retaliation an immediate decision slot.

Active attack reinforcement

  • A live outbound attack against any hostile player remains eligible for reinforcement even when its target is absent from the current nearby or border-target selector. Adequacy must include live committed troops and pending local intents, and reinforcement must emit only the missing amount up to the target-specific floor.

Defensive coverage accounting

  • When evaluating whether a candidate SAM adds protection, existing SAM coverage must be measured at each protected structure, not at the candidate SAM tile. Candidate coverage and existing coverage are separate geometric tests.

SAM-protected structure placement

  • New Cities, Factories, Ports, Missile Silos, and Defense Posts should prefer the closest legal placement to an existing own or allied SAM. A tile covered at the SAM's current level outranks one that becomes covered only after leveling; eventual maximum-level coverage outranks an uncovered tile. Distance to the covering SAM breaks ties inside each coverage tier. Candidate enrichment and probing must remain inside the existing 25-candidate budget.

Maritime-defense construction liveness

  • An active maritime-invasion signal may reserve construction while an emergency Port is genuinely unaffordable or under construction, and until the first Warship starts. It must not suppress all other structures when Warship automation is disabled, after a Warship exists, or after an affordable Port placement attempt fails for legality or integration reasons.

Stable naval interception assignments

  • A Warship assigned to an incoming transport must retain that transport ID and commanded interception target while both units remain live. Transport movement must not trigger repeated A* planning or redirect the same Warship on every naval-planner interval.
  • A planning pass may emit at most one immediate movement order per Warship. The assignment is released when the transport invasion ends or the assigned Warship is no longer active, after which uncovered live invasions may be planned normally.

Warship build-intent reconciliation

  • Initial deployment, retaliation, infestation-counter, and invasion-interceptor construction must share one pending Warship reservation. No path may emit another build while the client is still waiting for the accepted unit to appear. A newly visible Warship, including one under construction, confirms the intent; an intent that does not materialize releases after the normal six-game-tick reconciliation grace so a legitimate retry remains possible.

Outbound transport destination reservations

  • A successfully emitted transport intent reserves its destination and the surrounding configured boat-spread radius while the intent is pending and while a non-retreating active transport is traveling there. Parallel transports to spatially distinct opportunities remain legal. The reservation is released when the intent fails to materialize after the normal reconciliation grace or when the observed transport completes, disappears, becomes inactive, or retreats.

Preemptive SAM construction

  • Once any live enemy has completed a Missile Silo, placing the required SAM that best protects our structures becomes the absolute construction priority on every difficulty. Until that SAM is placed or already under construction, a failed, unaffordable, or rate-limited placement attempt must reserve the construction slot; economy, naval reserve, Defense Posts, and offensive Silos must not consume its gold. Enemy gold without a completed Silo may remain an anticipatory signal, but must not create this hard reservation.
  • A confirmed hostile-silo threat may authorize the first affordable SAM before any hostile missile has launched. The final construction gate must preserve that explicit authorization; ordinary ratio-driven SAM construction remains locked until a launch is observed.
  • Hostile Silo count must not create one SAM site per Silo. After the first useful SAM exists, increase its interceptor capacity and range before adding another site; another physical SAM is justified only by a structure-coverage gap that upgrading cannot close. A SAM already under construction must materialize before preemptive coverage geometry can justify another site; incomplete geometry must not produce adjacent duplicate level-one launchers.
  • Radioactive Terra Nullius remains an immediate expansion target, but it is not opening free land. If its attack cannot be emitted, its mere presence must not reactivate the opening-expansion veto on otherwise safe troop donations.

In-flight missile construction safety

  • No new structure or structure upgrade may be placed inside the configured outer blast radius of an active hostile Atom Bomb, Hydrogen Bomb, MIRV, or MIRV warhead. This final safety gate applies on every difficulty and includes SAM Launchers and Defense Posts.
  • An active hostile nuclear missile aimed at owned territory is a maximum-priority construction threat even when no economic structure lies inside its blast. The bot must first upgrade an existing SAM only when the upgrade improves the real interception schedule; otherwise it must reserve construction spending and seek a legal SAM site along the uncovered trajectory before maritime or economic construction. A second site is not justified when existing coverage is already sufficient.

Radioactive Terra Nullius expansion

  • Land and coastal attacks into Fallout may continue while leaving at least 42% of the player's current maximum troop capacity uncommitted. The radioactive reserve must remain isolated from ordinary Terra Nullius and player-attack reserves.
  • Fallout that belonged to another player with the same clan tag before impact is protected: the bot must neither expand into it by land nor target it with a boat. Because impact erases tile ownership and a clanmate can gain territory while a missile is in flight, the bot must re-evaluate an unclassified blast every tick. Once any blast overlaps clan territory, its complete outer-radius crater is quarantined until all fallout there clears. Since a generic Terra Nullius land attack can expand beyond its initial frontier, every such attack must be withheld during that quarantine and any already-active Terra Nullius attack must be cancelled before impact. Exact boat destinations inside the quarantined crater are also blocked.

VM-backed tests

  • Max-speed headless matches must yield one host task between simulated ticks so cooperative timers used by topology, route, and scoring work advance with the same scheduling boundary as live worker ticks. A tight microtask-only loop must not make a healthy planner appear permanently pending or suppress scored tribe attacks.
  • When a test receives objects from a node:vm sandbox, you should assert their scalar fields or normalize them before deep comparison. Objects from separate VM realms can fail strict deep equality even when their visible properties are identical.

Team donation legality

  • The Auto-Bot must not emit a troop donation when the authoritative player capability reports that the recipient is ineligible. After emitting a donation, it must honor the engine's recipient-specific donation cooldown before considering that teammate again. An invalid or cooldown-blocked donation must not consume a decision slot or intent budget that could otherwise be used for defense, expansion, or construction.
  • A troop donation must preserve at least the complete army of the strongest hostile Nation or Human sharing our border. The bot must not turn a locally superior army into an inferior one by exporting the defensive surplus; non-strategic and allied border armies retain their separate congestion reserve.
  • After spawn, two consecutive completed tribe-planner decisions with no free land, no actionable tribe, and no remaining land-expansion candidate establish an expansion-breakout reserve whenever either a hostile land border or a launch coast remains. While that state persists, troop donations are prohibited. Once a transport creates a hostile land border, a viable Nation or Human conquest may spend surplus down to the live growth/defense reserve instead of remaining permanently blocked by the higher production-peak reserve; the normal complete-capture floor remains mandatory.
